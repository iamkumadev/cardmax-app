FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between e-code and physical?

An e-code is a digital code you paste in directly. A physical card needs a clear photo of the front and back so we can read the number and PIN.

How long does verification take?

Usually under an hour for e-codes. Physical cards can take a little longer since a photo needs to be reviewed manually.

What happens if my card gets rejected?

You'll get a reason in your transaction history and can contact support directly from that trade to dispute it.
